Insurance Key Terms

Understand important insurance terms without complicated language.

💳

Premium

The amount a policyholder pays to maintain insurance coverage.

🛡️

Coverage

The specific risks, events, treatments, or situations protected by the policy.

📄

Policy

The legal contract between the policyholder and the insurance company.

📝

Claim

A formal request submitted to the insurer asking for payout when a covered event occurs.
