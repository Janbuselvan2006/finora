Eligibility Assessment Guide

1. Credit Score (CIBIL / Experian)

Scores range from 300 to 900. A score of 750+ demonstrates disciplined repayment behavior and unlocks competitive interest rates.

2. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)

Lenders prefer total monthly debt payments (existing loans + proposed EMI) to stay under 40–50% of net monthly income.

3. Employment & Income Stability

Continuous work experience of 1-2 years for salaried professionals; 2-3 years filed ITRs with positive cash flows for self-employed individuals.
