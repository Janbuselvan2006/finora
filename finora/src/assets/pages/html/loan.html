Understand Before You Apply

Key financial concepts explained in simple, human language.

%

Interest Rate

Represents the annual cost of borrowing. Fixed rates remain steady; floating rates fluctuate with economic benchmarks.

💡 Tip: Look at the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for total cost.
📅

EMI (Monthly Repayment)

Equated Monthly Installment is the regular fixed payment split between principal payback and interest fees.

💡 Tip: Keep your EMI under 40% of net monthly income.
⏳

Loan Tenure

The repayment duration. Longer tenure lowers your monthly EMI but increases the cumulative total interest paid.

💡 Tip: Balance monthly affordability with total interest.
🏷️

Processing Fees

Upfront administrative expenses charged by lenders for verification, underwriting, and loan disbursement.

💡 Tip: Always verify non-refundable fee clauses.
🎯

Eligibility Factors

Lenders evaluate income stability, credit score, age, and existing liabilities to assess repayment ability.

💡 Tip: A credit score above 700 expands lender choices.
📋

Documentation

Required verification paperwork including identity proofs, address validation, bank statements, and tax records.

💡 Tip: Verify names match across all submitted IDs.

      Key Assessment Factors

        📊

        Eligibility Assessment Factors

        Lenders evaluate multiple criteria to understand repayment reliability:

        1. Credit Score & Bureau History

        Scores typically range from 300 to 900 (e.g. CIBIL, Experian). A score of 700–750+ demonstrates strong credit discipline and unlocks lower interest rates.

        2. Debt-to-Income (FOIR / DTI) Ratio

        The Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR). Indian lenders prefer your total monthly EMI outgo to stay under 40–50% of net monthly income.

        3. Employment & Income Stability

        Salaried individuals typically require 1-2 years continuous work experience; self-employed individuals provide 2-3 years audited business accounts / ITR.

        4. Age & Career Longevity

        Borrowers typically range between 21 and 65 years. The remaining working years determine maximum allowable loan tenure.

        📋

        Comprehensive Document Checklist

        Common documentation requested by authorized Indian financial institutions:

        Identity & KYC Proof

        ✓ PAN Card (Mandatory) / Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID / Driving License

        Address & Residence Proof

        ✓ Aadhaar Card / Electricity Bill (within 3 months) / Registered Rent Agreement / Passport

        Income & Financial Records

        ✓ Latest 3–6 months salary slips / Last 6 months bank account statements / Form 16 or 2–3 years ITR (Income Tax Returns)

        Asset / Collateral Specifics

        ✓ Home Loan: Sale deed, approved building blueprint, NOC, Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
        ✓ Car Loan: Proforma dealer quotation / invoice
        ✓ Education Loan: College admission letter and detailed fee schedule

        %

        Interest & Repayment Explained

        Fixed vs Floating Rates

        Fixed rates guarantee exact monthly payments regardless of market changes. Floating rates fluctuate with central bank benchmark rates.

        Amortization Schedule Dynamics

        Early monthly payments consist predominantly of interest. As the principal balances drop over time, a greater share of each payment goes directly towards reducing principal.

        Prepayment & Foreclosure

        Making part-prepayments reduces total interest significantly. Many regulatory bodies mandate zero foreclosure penalties for individual floating-rate loans.
